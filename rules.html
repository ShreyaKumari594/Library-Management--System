LIBRARY GUIDELINES

Library Rules

Simple guidelines to keep our library peaceful and organized.

📚

Things to Remember

Please follow these rules while using the library and borrowing books.

01

Keep Your ID

Carry your valid library or student ID whenever you visit the library.

02

Return on Time

Return borrowed books on or before the due date to avoid overdue charges.

03

Maintain Silence

Keep your phone silent and maintain a quiet environment inside the library.

04

Handle Books Carefully

Do not fold, tear, highlight or damage pages of library books.

05

Food & Drinks

Avoid bringing food and beverages near books, shelves and reading areas.

06

Respect Others

Keep the library clean and respect other students and library staff.

💡

Important Note

Books must be returned in the same condition in which they were issued. Report any damaged book to the library staff immediately.
